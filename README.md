# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes agricultural data across different seasons to identify patterns, trends, relationships and variations in agricultural performance.

The analysis focuses on crop yield, production, environmental conditions, resource usage, water efficiency and economic performance.

## Problem Statement

Agricultural performance varies across seasons due to changes in environmental conditions, farming practices, resource availability and market conditions.

This project analyzes the given agricultural dataset to investigate seasonal differences and identify meaningful patterns, trends and relationships.

## Objectives

- Explore and understand the agricultural dataset.
- Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
- Compare agricultural performance across seasons.
- Analyze environmental conditions across seasons.
- Examine resource and water usage.
- Compare revenue, cost and profit.
- Identify relationships between variables.
- Perform statistical and visualization analysis.
- Identify unusual patterns and outliers.
- Develop evidence-based conclusions and recommendations.

## Dataset

The dataset contains 4,000 agricultural records and 28 attributes related to:

- Farm and geographical information
- Crop and season
- Environmental conditions
- Soil characteristics
- Fertilizer and pesticide usage
- Crop yield and production
- Market price, cost, revenue and profit
- Water usage and water efficiency
- Disease and pest risk
